When reporting a problem

It helps to include:

  • your device (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air) and iOS / iPadOS version;
  • the SIDtune version (shown in Settings);
  • the tune's path (for example /MUSICIANS/H/Hubbard_Rob/Commando.sid) and subtune, if the problem is with a particular tune;
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot or screen recording helps too.

Common questions

The library is empty. Where is the music?
No music ships with the app. Tap Download the HVSC to add the online High Voltage SID Collection (60,000+ tunes), or import your own .sid files, folders or zips with +. See Getting music.
Can I listen without a connection?
Yes. Tunes you have played are kept on your device, and Settings → Online HVSC → Download the whole collection stores all of them (about 350 MB). Imported files are always available offline.
There's no sound.
Check the volume. SIDtune plays even with the silent switch on.
Playback stutters on my older device.
Choose SIDLite under Settings → Sound → Emulation engine, and turn off Instant seeking. See Emulation settings.
A tune plays silence or garbage.
It may need the C64's KERNAL, BASIC and CHARGEN ROMs, which SIDtune cannot include for copyright reasons. If you own copies, add them under Settings → C64 ROMs. See C64 ROMs.
A tune stops too early or keeps going.
Its length is missing or wrong in HVSC's song-length database, so the default length from Settings is used.
How do I move my favorites to a new device, or to the SIDtune web app?
Use Settings → Backup → Save backup, then Restore a backup on the other device. The web app uses the same file. See Your data.
Does SIDtune collect any data about me?
No. There are no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads. See the Privacy Policy.
